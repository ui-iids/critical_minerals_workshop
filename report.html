Outcomes

Workshop report and outputs

Following the workshop, a comprehensive public report and a peer-reviewed journal publication will share the outcomes of the meeting. Both will be posted on this page.

What the workshop will produce

The two days are structured to generate content, not just discussion. Breakout groups draft material that feeds directly into a strategic roadmap for critical minerals and materials cyberinfrastructure.

01

Public workshop report

A comprehensive, openly available report capturing the state of practice, identified gaps, and prioritized recommendations.

02

Peer-reviewed publication

A journal article, co-authored with participants, presenting the roadmap to the broader scientific community.

Availability

Not yet published

The report will be released after the January 2027 meeting. This page will host the download link, the citation, and a persistent identifier (DOI).
