Getting there

Travel

By air

Three airports serve the region: Reagan National (DCA), Dulles International (IAD), and Baltimore/Washington International (BWI). DCA is closest to the venue.

By rail

Amtrak and regional rail serve Washington Union Station, roughly a 20–30 minute ride from the venue.

On site

Meals and facilities

Breakfast, lunch and refreshments are provided on both days.

Wi-Fi, presentation facilities, and breakout rooms are available at the venue.

Inclusion

Accessibility and accommodations

We are committed to a workshop that every invited participant can take part in fully. Please tell us by email at any time about accessibility needs, including mobility access, captioning, assistive listening, quiet space, dietary requirements, or caregiving support.

Day care needs

Participants are suggested to use day care services close to Carnegie Science if needed. The workshop will not reimburse such costs.
