Participation

Apply to attend

We expect to support approximately 80 participants from academia, government, and industry. Applications are collected through a short online form.

Who should apply

We welcome applications from researchers, data and software professionals, federal and state survey staff, and industry practitioners whose work touches critical minerals and materials data. Early-career researchers, students, and participants from institutions without existing cyberinfrastructure programs are strongly encouraged to apply.

What the application asks for

  • Name, affiliation, position, and contact information
  • A short statement of interest — what you would contribute and what you hope to gain
  • Areas of expertise relevant to the workshop focus areas
  • Whether you wish to give a lightning presentation
  • Travel support needs and any accessibility or dietary requirements

Selection

The organizing committee will review applications with the goal of assembling a group that spans sectors, career stages, disciplines, and institutions. Applicants will be notified by email.
